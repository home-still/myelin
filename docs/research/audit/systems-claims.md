## Defects found (ordered by likely damage to the plan)

1. **Zep LongMemEval_S 71.2% quoted without its backbone** (claim 2) — the plan cites "LongMemEval_S 71.2%" as a headline. In the Zep paper 71.2% is the **gpt-4o** row; the **gpt-4o-mini** row is 63.8%. A protocol that reproduces at 63.8% (the backbone most plans would use, and the one QueryLink's 69.8 uses) would under-claim the cited number. The 71.2% is real but backbone-conditional. **Damage: high** — it is a G1-benchmark-style target number in PLAN.md, and it only holds under gpt-4o.
2. **Mem0 "91% lower p95" / ">90% token reduction" quoted without the comparator** (claim 1b) — both are vs the **full-context** baseline on **LoCoMo** (gpt-4o-mini). They are not reductions vs any RAG/memory system; quoting them with no condition invites a false reading that Mem0 beats full-context on quality (it does not — full-context gets the highest J ≈73%, Mem0 67%). **Damage: medium-high** — the plan uses these to justify efficiency claims (§G2), so the condition (vs full-context, p95, LoCoMo) must be stated.
3. **Zep DMR 94.8 vs 98.2 apparent contradiction** — resolved: both correct, **different DMR backbones** (94.8=gpt-4-turbo for MemGPT parity; 98.2=gpt-4o-mini). The plan's 94.8 is the right one to pair with MemGPT 93.4 (both gpt-4-turbo). The other evidence file is not wrong, but it must cite the 98.2=gpt-4o-mini condition or it reads as a conflict. **Damage: medium** — a reviewer would flag an internal inconsistency; document both backbones.
4. **QueryLink "with no graph" beats Zep 69.8 vs 63.8** — numbers and backbone parity (both gpt-4o-mini, both LLM-as-a-Judge, same LongMemEval ~500-q set) hold, but the comparison is **cross-paper**: Zep's 63.8 comes from Zep's own paper's gpt-4o-mini row, not from a QueryLink-rerun. The "no graph" point is sound (QueryLink is flat alignment + chunking vs Zep's TKG). **Damage: low-medium** — as a like-for-like claim it's defensible but must note it is a cross-paper comparison on the same backbone/split.
5. **HippoRAG-2 synonym/extracted edge ratio called "~10×"** — actual is 1,125,951/140,830 ≈ **8.0×** (within order-of-magnitude; the plan's "~10×" appears in the prose only). Also the numbers are the **Llama-3.3-70B-Instruct** variant of Table 10, not the default GPT-4o-mini column. **Damage: minor** — no engineering decision changes, but cite the correct backbone column.
6. **HippoRAG "10–30× cheaper"** — accurate only for **online retrieval** (Appendix G, GPT-3.5-Turbo); offline indexing is actually ~10× slower and ~$15/10k passages more expensive. The plan says "10-30× cheaper than iterative IRCoT" with no 'online vs offline' qualifier. **Damage: minor** — a cost model built on it would overestimate savings from indexing.

**No fabricated numbers.** Every digit audited resolves to a real, quoted primary-source statement with a doc_id and line range; the defects are condition-stripping (71.2% backbone; Mem0 comparator), a same-backbone-but-cross-paper comparison (QueryLink/Zep), and minor ratio/backbone-attribution lints.
