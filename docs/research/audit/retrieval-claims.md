## Defects found

1. **Claim 3 (RocketQAv2 18.7→36.5) — MISATTRIBUTED.** 18.7 and 36.5 are two different baselines (BM25-anserini; BERT-large reranker). RocketQAv2-ERNIE's own BM25-rerank value is 40.1. As quoted, the plan credits RocketQAv2 with a weaker (36.5) result, understating it by ~3.6 MRR and mislabeling BERT-large's number as RocketQAv2's.
2. **Claim 5 ('hybrid +2.1 mAP over dense') — UNVERIFIABLE.** Not present in the Best-Practices-RAG paper (all mAP deltas are large, ~23 pts). The alpha=0.3 half of the claim is real and confirmed; the '+2.1 mAP' figure must be re-sourced or dropped.
3. **Claim 2 (ShardMemo '+3/+6.8 F1') — condition-stripped.** +6.8 is +6.76 and only for GPT-OSS-20B (appendix); main-backbone delta ≈5.9. '+3' is really +2.9/+3.1 over the simple learned router. Probe-budget parameters (B_probe=3 default, sweep {1,2,4,8}; K=10; S=40) omitted from the plan.
4. **Claim 4 (chunk 'best at 512') — wrong-edition + imprecise.** Table 3 lives in arXiv 2407.01219, not the cited EMNLP.981 PDF (Acc/EM/F1 metrics there). 512 is best only for faithfulness; relevancy peaks at 256 (97.78). Also it is a single-10-K toy eval with gpt-3.5-turbo as judge.
5. **Claim 1 — minor typo/labeling.** '10,834 ms' is a conversion typo of 10.834 ms; the '11.7 ms vs 794–1264 ms' figure belongs to LoCoMo Refined, a different benchmark.
6. Claims 6, 7, 8 CONFIRMED with clean quotes and no material condition-stripping.
